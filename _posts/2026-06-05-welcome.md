Welcome — this is where I'm collecting my notes, writeups, and study material as I work through cybersecurity, with a strong bias toward **Active Directory and Windows security**.

## What you'll find here

The plan is to publish across a few overlapping tracks:

- **Active Directory** — attack paths (Kerberoasting, delegation abuse, ACL & control-path analysis, ADCS ESC1–ESC15) and the hardening that closes them (Tiered Access, Microsoft Security Baselines, krbtgt rotation, Kerberos Armoring, Protected Users, LAPS).
- **Identity & cloud** — Entra ID / Azure AD security, Conditional Access, hybrid identity.
- **PKI / ADCS** — template misconfigurations and certificate lifecycle.
- **Threat detection** — Microsoft Defender for Identity (MDI), audit policy, PowerShell automation.
- **Pentesting writeups** — HTB, CRTE-style labs, and other study targets.

I try to cover both red-team and blue-team angles on the same topic — you can't really do one well without understanding the other.

If anything I write here turns out useful, wrong, or worth a follow-up, ping me on [GitHub](https://github.com/eereks) or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eslamerashed).
